A refreshing, mineral-rich green smoothie built on Earth Fare's clean food philosophy.

# 🥬 Ingredients
- @organic spinach{2%cups}
- @organic kale{1%cup}
- @frozen banana{1%whole}
- @fresh ginger{1%slice}
- @organic cucumber{0.5%whole}
- @coconut water{1.5%cups}
- @chia seeds{1%tbsp}

# 🌀 Instructions
I. Combine the @coconut water{1.5%cups} and leafy greens (@organic spinach{2%cups} and @organic kale{1%cup}) in the blender first. Blend on high until completely liquefied to avoid leafy chunks.
II. Add the @frozen banana{1%whole}, @fresh ginger{1%slice}, and @organic cucumber{0.5%whole}. Blend until smooth and creamy.
III. Stir in the @chia seeds{1%tbsp} and let sit for 2 minutes to allow them to expand slightly before serving.

💡 Chef's Additions & Troubleshooting
- **Texture Adjustments:** If too thick, add a splash more coconut water. If too thin, add 2-3 ice cubes or a tablespoon of rolled oats.
- **Ginger Intensity:** Fresh ginger varies in heat. Start with a thin slice (about 1/4 inch) and increase to taste.
- **Boost Nutritional Density:** Add a scoop of unflavored plant-based protein powder or a tablespoon of hemp hearts.
