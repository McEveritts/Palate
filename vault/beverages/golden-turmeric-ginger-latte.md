A soothing, warm, anti-inflammatory beverage crafted with organic turmeric and spices.

# ☀️ Ingredients
- @organic coconut milk{1.5%cups}
- @ground turmeric{1%tsp}
- @fresh ginger{1%slice}
- @ground cinnamon{0.25%tsp}
- @black pepper{1%pinch}
- @pure maple syrup{1%tsp}
- @coconut oil{0.5%tsp}

# 🌀 Instructions
I. In a small saucepan, combine @organic coconut milk{1.5%cups}, @ground turmeric{1%tsp}, @fresh ginger{1%slice}, @ground cinnamon{0.25%tsp}, @black pepper{1%pinch}, @pure maple syrup{1%tsp}, and @coconut oil{0.5%tsp}.
II. Whisk continuously over medium heat until warm but not boiling (about 5 minutes).
III. Pour through a fine mesh strainer into a mug to remove ginger slices. Froth with a hand frother if desired.

💡 Chef's Additions & Troubleshooting
- **Absorption Booster:** Never omit the black pepper; the piperine in black pepper increases curcumin (turmeric) absorption by up to 2000%.
- **Froth Quality:** A small dash of coconut oil or ghee helps create a creamy, luxurious micro-foam.
