A nutrient-dense, tropical berry smoothie packed with vitamins and natural antioxidants from Earth Fare.

# 🍓 Ingredients
- @frozen raspberries{1%cup}
- @frozen mango chunks{1%cup}
- @organic baby spinach{1%cup}
- @coconut water{1.5%cups}
- @chia seeds{1%tbsp}
- @raw honey{1%tsp}

# 🌀 Instructions
I. Place the @coconut water{1.5%cups} and @organic baby spinach{1%cup} in the blender first. Blend on high until the spinach is fully incorporated and smooth.
II. Add the frozen @frozen raspberries{1%cup} and @frozen mango chunks{1%cup}. 
III. Sprinkle in the @chia seeds{1%tbsp} and add the @raw honey{1%tsp}.
IV. Blend all ingredients on high for 45-60 seconds until rich and creamy. Let rest for 2 minutes before pouring to let chia seeds gel slightly.

💡 Chef's Additions & Troubleshooting
- **Green Blend:** Don't worry about the color; the combination of yellow mango, red raspberry, and green spinach makes a beautiful, vibrant deep purple.
- **Boost Protein:** Add a scoop of clean pea protein or a tablespoon of hemp hearts for sustained energy.
