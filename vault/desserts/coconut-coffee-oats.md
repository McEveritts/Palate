A creamy, cold, tiramisu-inspired overnight oat dessert with coconut milk and organic espresso.

# 🥥 Ingredients
- @rolled oats{0.5%cup}
- @chia seeds{1%tbsp}
- @coconut milk{0.5%cup}
- @cold brew coffee{0.25%cup}
- @maple syrup{1%tbsp}
- @greek yogurt{0.25%cup}
- @cocoa powder{1%tsp}

# 🍳 Instructions
I. In a jar or glass bowl, combine the @rolled oats{0.5%cup}, @chia seeds{1%tbsp}, @coconut milk{0.5%cup}, @cold brew coffee{0.25%cup}, and @maple syrup{1%tbsp}. Stir well.
II. Cover and refrigerate overnight (or for at least 4 hours) to allow the oats and chia seeds to fully absorb the liquids.
III. Before serving, stir the mixture. Top with a layer of thick @greek yogurt{0.25%cup} to mimic a tiramisu cream layer.
IV. Dust with @cocoa powder{1%tsp} through a small strainer. Eat chilled.

💡 Chef's Additions & Troubleshooting
- **Coffee Strength:** Adjust the ratio of coconut milk to cold brew to make the coffee flavor stronger or milder to suit your preference.
- **Vegan Swap:** Use coconut milk yogurt or cashew cream instead of Greek yogurt for a 100% plant-based option.
