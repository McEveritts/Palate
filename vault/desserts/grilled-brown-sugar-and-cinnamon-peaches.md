# 🍑 Ingredients

* @firm ripe peaches{4}
* @grass-fed butter{2%tbsp} (melted) or @refined coconut oil{2%tbsp}
* @organic brown sugar{3%tbsp}
* @ground cinnamon{1%tsp}
* @fine sea salt{1%pinch}

# 👨‍🍳 Instructions

I. Halve the @firm ripe peaches{4} and remove the pits, keeping the fruit intact.

II. In a small mixing bowl, whisk together the @grass-fed butter{2%tbsp}, @organic brown sugar{3%tbsp}, @ground cinnamon{1%tsp}, and @fine sea salt{1%pinch} until a smooth paste forms.

III. Generously brush the cut surface of each peach half with the brown sugar glaze.

IV. Preheat your grill or grill pan to medium-high heat. **Technique Note: Ensure the grates are lightly oiled with a high-smoke point oil to prevent the sugars from bonding to the metal.**

V. Place the peaches cut-side down onto the hot grill. Sear for 4 to 6 minutes without moving them.

VI. Carefully flip the peaches and grill for an additional 2 to 3 minutes. **Crucial Step: Monitor the heat closely; because of the high sugar content, the glaze can transition from caramelized to burnt very quickly.**

VII. Remove from heat and serve immediately while warm.

# 💡 Chef's Additions & Troubleshooting

* **The Maillard Reaction:** The deep golden-brown color and rich flavor are the results of the Maillard reaction and caramelization. To enhance this, ensure the peaches are patted dry before applying the glaze; excess moisture creates steam, which prevents the sugars from browning effectively.
* **Structural Integrity:** If your peaches are overly ripe, they may collapse on the grill. To remedy this, increase the heat to high and reduce the grilling time to "flash-sear" the exterior while keeping the center firm.
* **Acid Balance:** To cut through the intense sweetness of the brown sugar, serve these peaches with a dollop of @Greek yogurt{1%tbsp} or a squeeze of @fresh lime juice{1%tsp}. The acidity balances the palate and highlights the natural floral notes of the fruit.
