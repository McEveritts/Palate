# 🌿 Matcha Chia Seed Pudding

An elegant, nutrient-dense, and micro-balanced chilled dessert leveraging the high antioxidant profile of green tea and healthy fats from chia seeds.

## Ingredients
- 240ml Unsweetened almond milk (or coconut milk for rich mouthfeel)
- 40g Organic black chia seeds
- 5g Ceremonial grade matcha powder (sifted)
- 15ml Pure maple syrup
- 5ml Organic vanilla extract
- 1/4 tsp Ground cardamom (for a subtle, warming aromatic lift)
- Fresh raspberries and toasted coconut flakes for garnish

## Process
1. **Matcha Slurry:** In a medium bowl, whisk 30ml of the almond milk with the sifted matcha powder until completely smooth and free of lumps.
2. **Homogenize:** Slowly pour in the remaining 210ml of almond milk, whisking constantly. Whisk in the maple syrup, vanilla extract, and ground cardamom until thoroughly combined.
3. **Incorporate Seeds:** Add the chia seeds to the mixture. Whisk vigorously for 2 minutes to ensure even distribution and prevent clumping as the seeds begin to hydrate.
4. **Hydration Phase:** Let the mixture rest at room temperature for 10 minutes, then give it one final thorough whisk to break up any settling seeds.
5. **Cold Set:** Cover and refrigerate for a minimum of 2 hours (ideally overnight) to allow the seeds to fully hydrate and form a luxurious, spoonable pudding consistency.
6. **Plating & Garnish:** Spoon the set pudding into glass cups. Garnish with a few fresh raspberries and a sprinkle of toasted coconut flakes for contrasting texture.
