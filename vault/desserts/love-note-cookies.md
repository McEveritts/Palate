# 🍪 Ingredients

- @almond flour{2%cups}
- @maple syrup{1/3%cup}
- @refined coconut oil{1/4%cup, melted}
- @vanilla extract{1%tsp}
- @baking soda{1/2%tsp}
- @sea salt{1/4%tsp}
- @dark chocolate chips{1/2%cup}
- @Bonne Maman raspberry preserves{1/4%cup}

# 👩‍🍳 Instructions

I. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and line a professional baking sheet with parchment paper or a silicone mat.

II. In a large mixing bowl, whisk together the @refined coconut oil{1/4%cup}, @maple syrup{1/3%cup}, and @vanilla extract{1%tsp} until the mixture is smooth and glossy. **Technique Note:** Ensure the coconut oil is melted but not hot to prevent the syrup from separating.

III. Gradually stir in the @almond flour{2%cups}, @baking soda{1/2%tsp}, and @sea salt{1/4%tsp}. Fold in the @dark chocolate chips{1/2%cup} using a rubber spatula until just combined.

IV. Scoop approximately two tablespoons of dough and roll into a ball. Using your thumb, create a small indentation in the center of each cookie.

V. Carefully spoon a small amount of @Bonne Maman raspberry preserves{1/4%cup} into the center of each indentation. **Crucial Step:** Do not overfill the center, or the preserves will boil over the edges during baking, causing the cookies to spread unevenly.

VI. Bake for 10–12 minutes, or until the edges are lightly golden brown. 

VII. Allow the cookies to cool on the baking sheet for at least 10 minutes before transferring them to a wire rack.

# 💡 Chef's Additions & Troubleshooting

*   **The Science of Almond Flour:** Unlike wheat flour, almond flour lacks gluten, meaning these cookies will not "set" immediately upon removal from the oven. If you move them too early, they may collapse. Be patient with the cooling process to allow the fats to solidify and create a tender crumb.
*   **Preserve Consistency:** If your raspberry preserves are too fluid, simmer them in a small saucepan for 3-5 minutes to reduce the water content before filling. This prevents the "soggy bottom" effect and ensures a concentrated burst of fruit flavor.
*   **Temperature Control:** If the dough feels too soft to roll, chill it in the refrigerator for 15 minutes. This stabilizes the coconut oil, resulting in a more defined shape and a slightly chewier texture.
