# 🌿 Strawberry Shortcake Sammys

An elegant, grain-free interpretation of a summer classic. These "Sammys" utilize nutrient-dense almond flour and natural sweeteners to maintain a clean-eating profile without sacrificing the decadent texture of a traditional shortcake.

## 🍓 Ingredients

### The Shortcake Base
* @almond flour{2%cups}
* @baking powder{1%tsp}
* @sea salt{1/4%tsp}
* @melted coconut oil{3%tbsp}
* @maple syrup{3%tbsp}
* @vanilla extract{1%tsp}
* @unsweetened almond milk{2%tbsp}

### The Fresh Filling
* @fresh strawberries{2%cups, sliced}
* @lemon juice{1%tsp}
* @raw honey{1%tbsp}
* @chilled coconut cream{1%cup}
* @vanilla bean paste{1/2%tsp}

## 👩‍🍳 Instructions

I. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.

II. In a medium mixing bowl, whisk together the @almond flour{2%cups}, @baking powder{1%tsp}, and @sea salt{1/4%tsp}. 

III. Incorporate the @melted coconut oil{3%tbsp}, @maple syrup{3%tbsp}, @vanilla extract{1%tsp}, and @unsweetened almond milk{2%tbsp}. **Crucial Step:** Stir until just combined; over-mixing almond flour can lead to a dense, gummy texture rather than a light crumb.

IV. Scoop the batter into 6 equal mounds on the baking sheet. Gently press the tops to flatten them into small discs. Bake for 12–15 minutes or until the edges are golden brown. Allow them to cool completely on a wire rack.

V. While the cakes cool, combine @fresh strawberries{2%cups, sliced} with @lemon juice{1%tsp} and @raw honey{1%tbsp} in a glass bowl. Let the mixture sit for 20 minutes. **Technique Note:** This process, known as maceration, uses sugar and acid to draw out the natural juices of the berry, creating a built-in syrup.

VI. In a chilled bowl, whip the @chilled coconut cream{1%cup} and @vanilla bean paste{1/2%tsp} using a hand mixer on high speed until stiff peaks form.

VII. To assemble, place a dollop of whipped coconut cream on a cooled shortcake disc, top with a generous spoonful of macerated strawberries, and cover with a second disc to create the "Sammy."

## 💡 Chef's Additions & Troubleshooting

* **The Science of Maceration:** If your strawberries are particularly tart, increase the @raw honey{1%tbsp} slightly. The osmotic pressure created by the honey draws moisture from the strawberry cells, intensifying the flavor and creating a glossy glaze.
* **Cream Stability:** If your coconut cream is too liquid to whip, it likely wasn't chilled long enough. The saturated fats in coconut milk must be completely solidified (refrigerated for at least 24 hours) to trap air bubbles during whipping.
* **Nutrient Boost:** For added complexity and a lower glycemic index, fold 1 tablespoon of chia seeds into the strawberry mixture to create a jam-like consistency.
