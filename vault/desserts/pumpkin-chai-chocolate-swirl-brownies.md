Decadent, rich dark chocolate brownies swirled with a warm pumpkin chai spice cream cheese layer.

# 🍫 Ingredients
- @dark chocolate chips{1%cup}
- @coconut oil{0.5%cup}
- @coconut sugar{1%cup}
- @pasture-raised eggs{2%whole}
- @pure vanilla extract{1%tsp}
- @almond flour{0.75%cup}
- @cocoa powder{0.25%cup}
- @pumpkin puree{0.5%cup}
- @cream cheese{4%oz}
- @chai spice blend{1%tsp}

# 🍳 Instructions
I. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and line an 8x8 inch baking pan with parchment paper.
II. In a heatproof bowl, melt the @dark chocolate chips{1%cup} and @coconut oil{0.5%cup} together. Whisk in the @coconut sugar{1%cup}, @pasture-raised eggs{2%whole}, and @pure vanilla extract{1%tsp} until smooth.
III. Fold in the @almond flour{0.75%cup} and @cocoa powder{0.25%cup} until just combined. Pour into the prepared baking pan, reserving 3 tablespoons of batter for swirling.
IV. In a separate bowl, beat the @pumpkin puree{0.5%cup}, @cream cheese{4%oz}, and @chai spice blend{1%tsp} together until smooth.
V. Dollop the pumpkin mixture and the reserved brownie batter over the top. Use a butter knife to gently swirl them together, creating a marble pattern.
VI. Bake for 22-25 minutes until the edges are firm but the center remains slightly fudgy. Let cool completely before slicing.

💡 Chef's Additions & Troubleshooting
- **Fudgy Texture:** Do not overbake! Brownies continue to set as they cool. A toothpick inserted into the center should have a few moist crumbs.
- **Dairy-Free Alternative:** Use dairy-free cream cheese or thick coconut cream instead of standard cream cheese for the pumpkin layer.
