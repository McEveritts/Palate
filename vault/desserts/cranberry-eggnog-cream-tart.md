# 🌿 Sage's Clean-Eating Cranberry Eggnog Cream Tart

An elegant, refined holiday dessert that captures the nostalgic warmth of eggnog and the tart brightness of winter cranberries, reimagined with nutrient-dense, clean ingredients.

## 🥧 The Almond-Date Crust
- @almond flour{2%cups}
- @melted coconut oil{3%tbsp}
- @maple syrup{2%tbsp}
- @sea salt{1/4%tsp}

## 🥛 The Eggnog Cream Filling
- @raw cashews{1.5%cups} (soaked for 4 hours)
- @full-fat coconut cream{1/2%cup}
- @maple syrup{1/3%cup}
- @ground nutmeg{1/2%tsp}
- @ground cinnamon{1/4%tsp}
- @vanilla extract{1%tsp}
- @lemon juice{1%tbsp}

## 🍒 The Cranberry Compote
- @fresh cranberries{1%cup}
- @maple syrup{1%tbsp}
- @orange zest{1%tsp}
- @water{2%tbsp}

## 👨‍🍳 Preparation Steps

I. **Construct the Crust**: Combine @almond flour{2%cups}, @melted coconut oil{3%tbsp}, @maple syrup{2%tbsp}, and @sea salt{1/4%tsp} in a mixing bowl. Press the mixture firmly into the bottom and sides of a 9-inch tart pan.
**Technique Note:** Use the bottom of a flat glass to ensure an even, compacted base to prevent crumbling upon slicing.
Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 10–12 minutes until lightly golden. Allow to cool completely.

II. **Blend the Eggnog Filling**: Drain and rinse the @raw cashews{1.5%cups}. Place them in a high-speed blender with @full-fat coconut cream{1/2%cup}, @maple syrup{1/3%cup}, @ground nutmeg{1/2%tsp}, @ground cinnamon{1/4%tsp}, @vanilla extract{1%tsp}, and @lemon juice{1%tbsp}.
**Crucial Step:** Blend on high for 2–3 minutes until the texture is completely silk-smooth. Any remaining cashew grit will compromise the "cream" experience.

III. **Assemble**: Pour the eggnog filling into the cooled crust, smoothing the top with an offset spatula. Place the tart in the freezer for 30 minutes to set the surface.

IV. **Simmer the Cranberries**: In a small saucepan over medium heat, combine @fresh cranberries{1%cup}, @maple syrup{1%tbsp}, and @water{2%tbsp}. Simmer for 5–8 minutes until the berries burst and the liquid thickens into a syrup. Stir in @orange zest{1%tsp} and remove from heat.

V. **Final Garnish**: Spoon the cooled cranberry compote over the center of the tart, swirling outward with a toothpick for an elegant marbled effect. Refrigerate for at least 4 hours before serving.

## 💡 Chef's Additions & Troubleshooting

*   **The Science of Emulsification:** If your filling separates, it is likely due to the coconut cream's temperature. Ensure the coconut cream is at room temperature before blending to create a stable emulsion with the cashew fats.
*   **Pectin Activation:** Cranberries are naturally high in pectin. By simmering them with a small amount of water and maple syrup, you are activating this natural thickener, creating a glaze that holds its shape without the need for cornstarch or gelatin.
*   **Crust Integrity:** To prevent the almond crust from becoming too soft, ensure it is fully cooled before adding the filling. If the crust feels fragile, add an extra tablespoon of @almond flour{1%tbsp} to absorb excess moisture from the maple syrup.
