# 🌿 Lemon Chicken Pasta Salad

A bright, nutrient-dense assembly featuring whole grains and lean protein, balanced with a zesty citrus vinaigrette.

## 🍋 Ingredients

### The Base
- @whole grain rotini{12%oz}
- @grilled chicken breast, cubed{2%cups}
- @baby spinach{2%cups}
- @cherry tomatoes, halved{1%cup}
- @feta cheese, crumbled{1/4%cup}
- @fresh parsley, chopped{1/4%cup}

### The Vinaigrette
- @extra virgin olive oil{1/4%cup}
- @fresh lemon juice{3%tbsp}
- @lemon zest{1%tsp}
- @garlic, minced{2%cloves}
- @sea salt{1/2%tsp}
- @black pepper{1/4%tsp}

## 👨‍🍳 Instructions

I. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add @whole grain rotini{12%oz} and cook according to package directions. 
**Technique Note:** Aim for *al dente* (firm to the bite); overcooking the pasta will cause it to break down when tossed with the dressing.

II. While the pasta cooks, prepare the vinaigrette. In a small glass jar or bowl, whisk together @extra virgin olive oil{1/4%cup}, @fresh lemon juice{3%tbsp}, @lemon zest{1%tsp}, @garlic, minced{2%cloves}, @sea salt{1/2%tsp}, and @black pepper{1/4%tsp}. 
**Crucial Step:** Emulsify the dressing vigorously until the oil and citrus juice are fully combined and no longer separating.

III. Drain the pasta and rinse briefly under cold water to stop the cooking process and remove excess surface starch.

IV. In a large mixing bowl, combine the cooled pasta, @grilled chicken breast, cubed{2%cups}, @baby spinach{2%cups}, @cherry tomatoes, halved{1%cup}, and @fresh parsley, chopped{1/4%cup}.

V. Pour the lemon vinaigrette over the salad and toss gently to coat all ingredients evenly. 

VI. Fold in the @feta cheese, crumbled{1/4%cup} last to prevent the cheese from breaking down and clouding the dressing.

VII. Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes before serving to allow flavors to meld.

## 💡 Chef's Additions & Troubleshooting

*   **The Starch Absorption Principle:** Whole grain pasta is more porous than refined pasta. If the salad appears dry after refrigeration, fold in an additional tablespoon of @extra virgin olive oil{1%tbsp} or a squeeze of fresh lemon. The grains absorb the dressing over time, which can mute the brightness of the acidity.
*   **Oxidation Control:** If preparing this 24 hours in advance, add the @baby spinach{2%cups} and @fresh parsley, chopped{1/4%cup} immediately before serving. The citric acid in the dressing will eventually break down the chlorophyll in the greens, leading to a wilted appearance and a duller green hue.
*   **Texture Contrast:** For an added layer of complexity, incorporate toasted @pine nuts{2%tbsp} or @sunflower seeds{2%tbsp}. This introduces a lipid-rich crunch that offsets the softness of the pasta and chicken.
