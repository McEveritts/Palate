# 🌿 Pumpkin and Béchamel Lasagna

This sophisticated autumn ensemble balances the earthy sweetness of organic pumpkin with the velvet richness of a classic Béchamel. Designed for the 'Palate' standard, this recipe utilizes clean, whole ingredients to elevate a seasonal favorite.

# 🛒 Ingredients

**For the Béchamel Sauce**
* @grass-fed butter{4%tbsp}
* @organic all-purpose flour{1/4%cup}
* @organic whole milk{3%cups}
* @ground nutmeg{1/4%tsp}
* @sea salt{1/2%tsp}
* @white pepper{1/4%tsp}

**For the Pumpkin Filling**
* @organic pumpkin puree{15%oz}
* @whole milk ricotta{15%oz}
* @large egg{1}
* @fresh sage, minced{1%tbsp}
* @grated parmesan cheese{1/4%cup}
* @sea salt{to taste}
* @black pepper{to taste}

**For Assembly**
* @whole grain lasagna noodles{12%oz}
* @shredded mozzarella cheese{2%cups}
* @grated parmesan cheese{1/4%cup}

# 🍳 Instructions

I. **Prepare the Béchamel:** Melt the @grass-fed butter{4%tbsp} in a heavy-bottomed saucepan over medium heat. Stir in the @organic all-purpose flour{1/4%cup} and whisk continuously for 2 minutes to cook out the raw flour taste without browning. Gradually pour in the @organic whole milk{3%cups}, whisking vigorously to ensure no lumps form. Simmer on low until thickened. Stir in @ground nutmeg{1/4%tsp}, @sea salt{1/2%tsp}, and @white pepper{1/4%tsp}.
**Technique Note:** Maintain a steady whisking motion to create a stable emulsion between the fat and the liquid.

II. **Create the Pumpkin Mixture:** In a medium mixing bowl, combine the @organic pumpkin puree{15%oz}, @whole milk ricotta{15%oz}, and @grated parmesan cheese{1/4%cup}. Beat in the @large egg{1} until smooth. Fold in the @fresh sage, minced{1%tbsp} and season with @sea salt{to taste} and @black pepper{to taste}.
**Crucial Step:** Ensuring the egg is fully incorporated prevents the ricotta from separating during the baking process, maintaining a creamy structure.

III. **Assemble the Lasagna:** Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C). Spread a thin layer of Béchamel sauce at the bottom of a 9x13 inch baking dish. Layer in the following order: @whole grain lasagna noodles{12%oz}, a generous layer of the pumpkin mixture, a drizzle of Béchamel, and a sprinkle of @shredded mozzarella cheese{2%cups}. Repeat these layers until all ingredients are utilized, finishing with a final layer of noodles topped with the remaining Béchamel and the remaining @grated parmesan cheese{1/4%cup}.

IV. **Bake and Set:** Cover the dish with foil and bake for 25 minutes. Remove the foil and bake for an additional 15-20 minutes until the cheese is golden-brown and bubbling.
**Technique Note:** Let the lasagna rest for 15 minutes before slicing to allow the internal structure to set, preventing the layers from sliding.

# 💡 Chef's Additions & Troubleshooting

* **The Maillard Reaction:** For a deeper flavor profile, lightly sauté the minced sage in butter before adding it to the pumpkin mixture. This releases the fat-soluble aromatic compounds in the sage, enhancing the overall earthiness.
* **Texture Calibration:** If the Béchamel becomes too thick, whisk in a tablespoon of warm milk at a time. A perfect Béchamel should coat the back of a spoon (nappe consistency) without being gluey.
* **Noodle Hydration:** When using whole grain noodles, ensure they are cooked *al dente*. They will continue to absorb moisture from the Béchamel and pumpkin filling during the oven phase; overcooking them initially will lead to a mushy texture.
