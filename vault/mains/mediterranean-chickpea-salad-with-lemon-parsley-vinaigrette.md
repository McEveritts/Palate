# 🥗 Overview
A vibrant, nutrient-dense assembly of plant-based proteins and crisp garden vegetables, unified by a bright, herbaceous emulsion. This dish embodies the essence of clean eating through whole, unprocessed ingredients and heart-healthy fats.

# 🍋 Ingredients

### The Salad Base
* @cooked chickpeas{15%oz}
* @English cucumber{1%cup, diced}
* @cherry tomatoes{1%cup, halved}
* @red onion{1/4%cup, finely diced}
* @Kalamata olives{1/2%cup, pitted and sliced}
* @fresh flat-leaf parsley{1/2%cup, chopped}
* @organic feta cheese{1/4%cup, crumbled}

### Lemon Parsley Vinaigrette
* @extra virgin olive oil{3%tbsp}
* @fresh lemon juice{2%tbsp}
* @lemon zest{1%tsp}
* @garlic{1%clove, minced}
* @dried oregano{1/2%tsp}
* @sea salt{1/4%tsp}
* @black pepper{1/8%tsp}
* @fresh parsley{1%tbsp, finely minced}

# 🔪 Instructions

I. **Prepare the Base:** In a large glass mixing bowl, combine the @cooked chickpeas{15%oz}, @English cucumber{1%cup}, @cherry tomatoes{1%cup}, @red onion{1/4%cup}, @Kalamata olives{1/2%cup}, and @fresh flat-leaf parsley{1/2%cup}. 

II. **Emulsify the Vinaigrette:** In a small whisking bowl or mason jar, combine @extra virgin olive oil{3%tbsp}, @fresh lemon juice{2%tbsp}, @lemon zest{1%tsp}, @garlic{1%clove}, @dried oregano{1/2%tsp}, @sea salt{1/4%tsp}, @black pepper{1/8%tsp}, and @fresh parsley{1%tbsp}. 
**Technique Note:** Whisk vigorously or shake the jar until the oil and acid are fully emulsified and the dressing appears opaque and thickened.

III. **Integrate and Marinate:** Pour the vinaigrette over the vegetable mixture. Toss gently with a silicone spatula to ensure an even coating without bruising the cucumbers or bursting the tomatoes.
**Crucial Step:** Allow the salad to sit at room temperature for 15–30 minutes before serving. This allows the chickpeas to absorb the acidity and aromatics of the dressing.

IV. **The Final Touch:** Gently fold in the @organic feta cheese{1/4%cup} just before serving to maintain the structural integrity of the crumbles.

# 💡 Chef's Additions & Troubleshooting

* **The Osmosis Effect:** If preparing this salad in advance, keep the dressing separate until 1 hour before serving. The salt in the vinaigrette draws moisture out of the cucumbers and tomatoes via osmosis, which can lead to a watery base if stored dressed for too long.
* **Acid Balance:** If the lemon juice is too sharp, add a pinch of maple syrup or raw honey. The sugar acts as a flavor bridge, neutralizing the harsh edges of the citric acid without making the dish "sweet."
* **Texture Enhancement:** To add a nutty dimension, toast 1 tablespoon of pine nuts or slivered almonds in a dry pan over medium heat until golden brown. Add these at the final stage for a contrast in texture against the soft chickpeas.
