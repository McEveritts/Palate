A savory, quick-cooking meal prep staple featuring organic grass-fed ground beef and colorful sweet bell peppers.

# 🥩 Ingredients
- @grass-fed ground beef{1%lb}
- @sweet bell peppers{2%whole}
- @yellow onion{1%whole}
- @garlic cloves{3%whole}
- @jasmine rice{1%cup}
- @tamari soy sauce{2%tbsp}
- @sesame oil{1%tbsp}
- @green onions{2%whole}

# 🍳 Instructions
I. Rinse the @jasmine rice{1%cup} thoroughly under cold water. Combine with 1.75 cups of water in a pot. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and let simmer for 15 minutes.
II. While the rice is cooking, dice the @yellow onion{1%whole} and slice the @sweet bell peppers{2%whole} into thin strips. Mince the @garlic cloves{3%whole}.
III. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the @grass-fed ground beef{1%lb} and cook, breaking it up with a wooden spoon, until browned (about 5-7 minutes). Drain any excess fat.
IV. Push the beef to one side of the skillet. Add the @sesame oil{1%tbsp} to the empty side, then toss in the onions, peppers, and garlic. Sauté for 4-5 minutes until the vegetables are tender-crisp.
V. Drizzle the @tamari soy sauce{2%tbsp} over the beef and vegetables. Toss everything together in the skillet for 1-2 minutes until thoroughly coated and hot.
VI. Spoon a base of fluffy jasmine rice into bowls, top with the beef and pepper mixture, and garnish with sliced @green onions{2%whole}.

💡 Chef's Additions & Troubleshooting
- **Spice Upgrade:** Add a tablespoon of sriracha or half a teaspoon of crushed red pepper flakes to the tamari sauce for a kick.
- **Grain Alternates:** Swap jasmine rice for organic brown rice or quinoa to increase dietary fiber.
