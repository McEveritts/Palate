# 🌿 Citrus-Glazed Chicken and Apricot Kabobs

A sophisticated blend of bright acidity and sun-dried sweetness, these kabobs exemplify the clean-eating philosophy by pairing lean protein with nutrient-dense fruits and aromatics.

## 🛒 Ingredients

### The Marinade & Glaze
- @extra virgin olive oil{2%tbsp}
- @fresh orange juice{1/4%cup}
- @raw honey{2%tbsp}
- @fresh ginger, grated{1%tsp}
- @garlic, minced{2%cloves}
- @sea salt{1/2%tsp}
- @black pepper{1/4%tsp}

### The Kabobs
- @boneless skinless chicken breast, cubed{1.5%lbs}
- @dried unsulfured apricots, halved{1/2%cup}
- @red onion, cut into chunks{1%medium}
- @red bell pepper, cut into chunks{1%large}

## 🍳 Instructions

I. In a medium glass bowl, whisk together @extra virgin olive oil{2%tbsp}, @fresh orange juice{1/4%cup}, @raw honey{2%tbsp}, @fresh ginger, grated{1%tsp}, @garlic, minced{2%cloves}, @sea salt{1/2%tsp}, and @black pepper{1/4%tsp} until emulsified.

II. Reserve 1/4 cup of the mixture for glazing. Place the @boneless skinless chicken breast, cubed{1.5%lbs} into a shallow dish or zip-top bag and pour the remaining marinade over the meat. **Technique Note:** Allow the chicken to marinate for at least 30 minutes, but no longer than 4 hours, to prevent the citrus acid from breaking down the protein fibers too aggressively, which can result in a mealy texture.

III. Thread the marinated chicken, @dried unsulfured apricots, halved{1/2%cup}, @red onion, cut into chunks{1%medium}, and @red bell pepper, cut into chunks{1%large} onto skewers, alternating colors and textures for visual balance.

IV. Preheat your grill or grill pan to medium-high heat. Lightly oil the grates to prevent sticking.

V. Place kabobs on the heat. Grill for 5–7 minutes per side. **Crucial Step:** During the final 3 minutes of cooking, brush the reserved glaze generously over the chicken and apricots. This allows the sugars in the honey and fruit to caramelize without burning.

VI. Remove from heat once the chicken reaches an internal temperature of 165°F (74°C). Let rest for 5 minutes before serving.

## 💡 Chef's Additions & Troubleshooting

*   **Maillard Reaction Optimization:** For the most professional sear, pat the chicken slightly dry before the final grilling stage if the marinade is too watery. The interaction between amino acids and reducing sugars (from the honey) creates the characteristic golden-brown crust and deep savory flavor.
*   **Apricot Rehydration:** If your @dried unsulfured apricots{1/2%cup} feel overly tough, soak them in warm water for 10 minutes and pat dry before skewering. This ensures they soften at the same rate the chicken cooks, preventing them from remaining leathery.
*   **Acid Balance:** If the glaze tastes too sweet, add a squeeze of fresh lime juice immediately after removing the kabobs from the grill. The citric acid cuts through the honey's richness and brightens the overall flavor profile.
