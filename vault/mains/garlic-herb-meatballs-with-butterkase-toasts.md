# 🌿 Garlic Herb Meatballs with Butterkase Toasts

An elegant, nutrient-dense pairing of savory, herb-infused protein and the rich, nutty profile of Butterkase cheese on artisan sourdough.

## 🥖 Ingredients

### For the Meatballs
* @ground turkey{1%lb}
* @egg{1%large}
* @almond flour{1/4%cup}
* @fresh parsley{2%tbsp} (finely chopped)
* @fresh oregano{1%tbsp} (finely chopped)
* @garlic{3%cloves} (minced)
* @sea salt{1%tsp}
* @black pepper{1/2%tsp}
* @olive oil{1%tbsp}

### For the Butterkase Toasts
* @sourdough bread{4%slices}
* @butterkase cheese{4%oz} (shredded)
* @grass-fed butter{2%tbsp} (softened)
* @garlic{1%clove} (grated)

## 🍳 Instructions

I. In a large mixing bowl, combine the @ground turkey{1%lb}, @egg{1%large}, @almond flour{1/4%cup}, @fresh parsley{2%tbsp}, @fresh oregano{1%tbsp}, @garlic{3%cloves}, @sea salt{1%tsp}, and @black pepper{1/2%tsp}. 

**Technique Note:** Mix by hand until just combined. **Crucial Step:** Do not overwork the meat, as over-mixing leads to dense, rubbery meatballs; keep the texture light and airy.

II. Roll the mixture into approximately 16-20 evenly sized spheres (about 1 inch in diameter).

III. Heat @olive oil{1%tbsp} in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the meatballs and brown on all sides for 8-10 minutes until the internal temperature reaches 165°F (74°C). Remove from heat and set aside.

IV. In a small bowl, cream together the @grass-fed butter{2%tbsp} and grated @garlic{1%clove}. Spread the mixture evenly across the @sourdough bread{4%slices}.

V. Top each slice of bread with a generous portion of @butterkase cheese{4%oz}.

VI. Place the toasts on a baking sheet and broil on high for 2-3 minutes, or until the cheese is bubbling and golden brown.

VII. Serve the garlic herb meatballs atop or alongside the warm Butterkase toasts.

## 💡 Chef's Additions & Troubleshooting

* **The Maillard Reaction:** To achieve a professional crust on your meatballs, ensure the skillet is shimmering hot before adding the meat. This triggers the Maillard reaction, creating complex flavor compounds and a deep golden-brown sear that contrasts with the tender interior.
* **Cheese Stability:** Butterkase is a semi-hard cheese with a high fat content. To prevent the cheese from "splitting" (where oil separates from the protein) during broiling, ensure the oven is fully preheated. A rapid, high-heat melt preserves the emulsion of the cheese for a creamier mouthfeel.
* **Texture Adjustment:** If the meatball mixture feels too wet (depending on the fat content of your turkey), add an extra tablespoon of @almond flour{amount}. The almond flour acts as a clean-eating binder, absorbing excess moisture without introducing refined grains.
