# 🌿 Sage's Clean-Eating Taco Casserole

A nutrient-dense, sophisticated take on the classic comfort dish. By replacing refined tortillas with a protein-rich quinoa base and utilizing organic, whole ingredients, we maintain the bold flavors of a taco while prioritizing cellular wellness.

# 🥗 Ingredients

* @grass-fed ground beef{1%lb}
* @cooked organic quinoa{3%cups}
* @organic black beans, rinsed{15%oz}
* @organic sweet corn{1%cup}
* @organic chunky salsa{2%cups}
* @ground cumin{1%tsp}
* @smoked paprika{1%tsp}
* @garlic powder{1%tsp}
* @sea salt{1/2%tsp}
* @organic shredded sharp cheddar{1%cup}
* @fresh cilantro, chopped{1/4%cup}
* @ripe avocado, sliced{1%whole}
* @extra virgin olive oil{1%tbsp}

# 🍳 Instructions

I. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C). Lightly grease a 9x9 inch ceramic baking dish with @extra virgin olive oil{1%tbsp}.

II. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add @grass-fed ground beef{1%lb}. 
**Technique Note:** Allow the beef to sear undisturbed for 3 minutes to achieve a deep brown crust; this utilizes the Maillard reaction to maximize savory depth.

III. Stir in @ground cumin{1%tsp}, @smoked paprika{1%tsp}, @garlic powder{1%tsp}, and @sea salt{1/2%tsp}. Cook until beef is fully browned, then drain any excess rendered fat.

IV. In a large mixing bowl, combine the seasoned beef with @cooked organic quinoa{3%cups}, @organic black beans, rinsed{15%oz}, @organic sweet corn{1%cup}, and @organic chunky salsa{2%cups}. Fold gently to ensure even distribution of flavors.

V. Transfer the mixture into the prepared baking dish, spreading it evenly with a spatula. Top with @organic shredded sharp cheddar{1%cup}.

VI. Bake for 20–25 minutes, or until the cheese is bubbly and slightly golden. 
**Crucial Step:** Do not overbake; the goal is to melt the cheese and heat the filling without drying out the quinoa.

VII. Remove from the oven and let rest for 5 minutes. Garnish with @fresh cilantro, chopped{1/4%cup} and slices of @ripe avocado{1%whole} before serving.

# 💡 Chef's Additions & Troubleshooting

* **Emulsion & Moisture Control:** If the casserole appears too dry before baking, stir in 2 tablespoons of organic beef bone broth. The gelatin in the broth creates a silkier mouthfeel and prevents the quinoa from becoming grainy under high heat.
* **The Acid Balance:** If the salsa is overly sweet, add a squeeze of fresh lime juice to the filling. The citric acid cuts through the richness of the grass-fed beef and sharp cheddar, brightening the overall flavor profile.
* **Texture Optimization:** For those seeking a "crunch" without processed chips, top the casserole with toasted pepitas (pumpkin seeds) during the last 5 minutes of baking for a nutrient-dense, textural contrast.
