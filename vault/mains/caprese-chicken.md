# 🌿 Caprese Chicken
An elegant, clean-eating interpretation of the classic Italian salad, reimagined as a protein-rich main. This dish emphasizes fresh, whole ingredients and a balance of acidity and creaminess.

# 🍅 Ingredients
* @organic chicken breasts{4%pieces}
* @extra virgin olive oil{2%tbsp}
* @fresh mozzarella pearls{1%cup}
* @cherry tomatoes, halved{1%cup}
* @fresh basil leaves, chiffonade{1/4%cup}
* @balsamic glaze{2%tbsp}
* @garlic cloves, minced{3%pieces}
* @sea salt{1%tsp}
* @black pepper{1/2%tsp}

# 🍳 Preparation

I. Pat the @organic chicken breasts{4%pieces} dry with a paper towel. Season evenly with @sea salt{1%tsp} and @black pepper{1/2%tsp}. 

II. Heat @extra virgin olive oil{2%tbsp} in a large oven-safe skillet over medium-high heat. Add the chicken and sear for 5-7 minutes per side until a golden-brown crust forms.
**Technique Note:** Ensure the pan is shimmering hot before adding the meat to achieve a proper Maillard reaction, which develops the deep, savory flavor profile.

III. Reduce heat to medium and stir in the @garlic cloves, minced{3%pieces}, sautéing for 60 seconds until fragrant but not browned.

IV. Top each chicken breast with @cherry tomatoes, halved{1%cup} and @fresh mozzarella pearls{1%cup}. 
**Crucial Step:** Distribute the cheese and tomatoes evenly across the surface to prevent the cheese from sliding off during the melting process.

V. Transfer the skillet to a preheated oven at 400°F (200°C) for 8-10 minutes, or until the chicken reaches an internal temperature of 165°F and the mozzarella is bubbly.

VI. Remove from the oven and immediately garnish with @fresh basil leaves, chiffonade{1/4%cup} and a precise drizzle of @balsamic glaze{2%tbsp}.

## 💡 Chef's Additions & Troubleshooting

* **Moisture Control:** If the cherry tomatoes release too much liquid in the pan, avoid crowding the chicken. Leaving space between the breasts ensures the chicken sears rather than steams, maintaining the integrity of the crust.
* **The Acid Balance:** To elevate the flavor, whisk a drop of lemon juice into the @balsamic glaze{2%tbsp}. The addition of citric acid cuts through the richness of the mozzarella, brightening the overall palate of the dish.
* **Cheese Selection:** For a lower-fat alternative, use a part-skim mozzarella. To prevent it from becoming rubbery, ensure the oven is fully preheated; a rapid melt preserves the creamy texture of the cheese.
