# 🌿 Ingredients

### For the Stuffing
* @extra virgin olive oil{2%tbsp}
* @yellow onion{1/4%cup, minced}
* @carrot{1/4%cup, finely diced}
* @celery{1/4%cup, finely diced}
* @garlic{2%cloves, minced}
* @cooked wild rice{2%cups}
* @grated Asiago cheese{1/2%cup}
* @fresh thyme{1%tsp, minced}
* @fresh parsley{2%tbsp, chopped}
* @sea salt{1/2%tsp}
* @black pepper{1/4%tsp}

### For the Chicken
* @boneless skinless chicken breasts{4%units}
* @avocado oil{1%tbsp}
* @sea salt{to taste}
* @black pepper{to taste}

# 🍳 Preparation

I. Heat @extra virgin olive oil{2%tbsp} in a medium skillet over medium heat. Add @yellow onion{1/4%cup}, @carrot{1/4%cup}, and @celery{1/4%cup}. Sauté for 5–7 minutes until softened. 

II. Stir in @garlic{2%cloves} and @fresh thyme{1%tsp}, cooking for another 60 seconds until fragrant. Remove from heat.

III. In a mixing bowl, combine the sautéed aromatics with @cooked wild rice{2%cups}, @grated Asiago cheese{1/2%cup}, @fresh parsley{2%tbsp}, @sea salt{1/2%tsp}, and @black pepper{1/4%tsp}. Fold gently until the cheese begins to melt slightly from the residual heat.

IV. Pat @boneless skinless chicken breasts{4%units} dry with paper towels. Using a sharp paring knife, cut a pocket into the thickest part of each breast, being careful not to cut all the way through. **Technique Note:** Cutting a "pocket" rather than splitting the breast ensures the chicken remains juicy and the stuffing stays contained.

V. Divide the rice mixture evenly among the chicken pockets, pressing gently to fill. Secure the opening with 2–3 toothpicks if necessary. Season the exterior of the chicken with @sea salt{to taste} and @black pepper{to taste}.

VI. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C). Heat @avocado oil{1%tbsp} in an oven-safe skillet over medium-high heat. 

VII. Sear the chicken for 3–4 minutes per side until a golden-brown crust forms. **Crucial Step:** Do not overcrowd the pan; sear in batches if necessary to maintain high heat and avoid steaming the meat.

VIII. Transfer the skillet to the oven and roast for 12–15 minutes, or until the internal temperature of the chicken reaches 165°F (74°C). Let the chicken rest for 5 minutes before serving.

# 💡 Chef's Additions & Troubleshooting

* **The Maillard Reaction:** Searing the chicken in avocado oil (which has a high smoke point) triggers the Maillard reaction, creating complex flavor compounds and a deep brown crust. This not only enhances the taste but helps the chicken retain internal moisture during the roasting phase.
* **Managing Carryover Cooking:** Remove the chicken from the oven when it hits 160°F (71°C). The internal temperature will continue to rise to 165°F during the resting period. This prevents the breast meat from becoming stringy or dry.
* **Rice Texture Control:** If your cooked wild rice is too dry, stir in 1 tablespoon of @low-sodium chicken broth{1%tbsp} into the stuffing mixture to ensure a creamy consistency once the Asiago melts.
