# 🌿 Vegan Falafel Bowl

An elegant, nutrient-dense assembly of house-made herb falafel, fluffy quinoa, and a zesty tahini emulsion. This bowl emphasizes clean, whole-food ingredients for a balanced, professional-grade lunch.

## 🥗 Ingredients

### For the Falafel
* @dried chickpeas{1%cup} (soaked for 24 hours, not canned)
* @fresh parsley{1%cup}
* @fresh cilantro{1%cup}
* @garlic cloves{3%pcs}
* @red onion{1/2%pcs}
* @ground cumin{1%tsp}
* @ground coriander{1%tsp}
* @lemon juice{2%tbsp}
* @extra virgin olive oil{3%tbsp}
* @sea salt{1%tsp}

### For the Bowl Base & Toppings
* @cooked quinoa{2%cups}
* @english cucumber{1%pc} (diced)
* @cherry tomatoes{1%cup} (halved)
* @shredded carrots{1%cup}
* @red cabbage{1%cup} (thinly sliced)

### For the Tahini Drizzle
* @tahini{1/4%cup}
* @lemon juice{1%tbsp}
* @maple syrup{1%tsp}
* @warm water{2%tbsp}

## 👨‍🍳 Instructions

I. **Prepare the Falafel Base**: Drain and rinse the @dried chickpeas{1%cup}. Place them in a food processor along with @fresh parsley{1%cup}, @fresh cilantro{1%cup}, @garlic cloves{3%pcs}, @red onion{1/2%pcs}, @ground cumin{1%tsp}, @ground coriander{1%tsp}, @lemon juice{2%tbsp}, and @sea salt{1%tsp}. 

**Crucial Step:** Process the mixture in short pulses. Do not over-blend into a paste; the texture should be coarse and granular to ensure the falafel maintains its structural integrity during cooking.

II. **Shape and Sear**: Form the mixture into small, uniform patties or spheres. Heat @extra virgin olive oil{3%tbsp} in a cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat. Fry the patties for 3-4 minutes per side until a deep golden-brown crust forms.

**Technique Note:** Use a flat-bottomed spatula to gently press the patties, maximizing surface contact with the pan to trigger the Maillard reaction for superior flavor.

III. **Emulsify the Dressing**: In a small glass bowl, whisk together @tahini{1/4%cup}, @lemon juice{1%tbsp}, and @maple syrup{1%tsp}. Slowly incorporate @warm water{2%tbsp} one tablespoon at a time until the sauce reaches a smooth, pourable consistency.

IV. **Assemble the Bowl**: Divide @cooked quinoa{2%cups} evenly among four bowls. Arrange @english cucumber{1%pc}, @cherry tomatoes{1%cup}, @shredded carrots{1%cup}, and @red cabbage{1%cup} in distinct sections atop the quinoa.

V. **Final Plating**: Place three warm falafel patties in the center of each bowl and generously drizzle the tahini emulsion over the entire arrangement.

## 💡 Chef's Additions & Troubleshooting

*   **The Chickpea Paradox**: Always use soaked dried chickpeas rather than canned. Canned chickpeas contain excess moisture and lack the necessary starch structure, which often causes the falafel to crumble or disintegrate upon frying.
*   **Tahini Seizing**: If your tahini sauce thickens abruptly (seizes), it is a result of the fats separating from the water-based lemon juice. Continue whisking in warm water; the heat helps re-emulsify the lipids, restoring the creamy texture.
*   **Acid Balancing**: If the bowl feels "heavy," add a pinch of sumac to the sliced red onions before adding them to the bowl. The tart, citrusy profile of sumac cuts through the richness of the tahini and the fried chickpeas.
