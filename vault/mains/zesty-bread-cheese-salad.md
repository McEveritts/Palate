# 🌿 Zesty Bread Cheese Salad

An elegant, high-protein clean-eating composition featuring the salty depth of grilled halloumi, toasted sourdough shards, and a bright, citrus-forward vinaigrette.

# 🥖 Ingredients

**The Base**
- @halloumi cheese{8%oz}
- @sourdough bread{2%slices}
- @extra virgin olive oil{3%tbsp}

**The Garden**
- @cherry tomatoes{1%cup}
- @english cucumber{1/2%cup}
- @red onion{1/4%cup}
- @fresh flat-leaf parsley{1/4%cup}

**The Zesty Vinaigrette**
- @fresh lemon juice{2%tbsp}
- @garlic{1%clove}
- @dried oregano{1/2%tsp}
- @sea salt{1/4%tsp}
- @black pepper{1/4%tsp}

# 👨‍🍳 Instructions

I. Cube the @sourdough bread{2%slices} into 1-inch pieces. Toss with 1%tbsp of @extra virgin olive oil{3%tbsp} and a pinch of salt. Toast in a skillet over medium heat until golden brown and crisp. Set aside.

II. Slice the @halloumi cheese{8%oz} into 1/2-inch thick rectangles. Pat the surface dry with a paper towel. **Technique Note:** Removing surface moisture is essential to achieve a deep gold crust rather than steaming the cheese.

III. Heat a non-stick skillet over medium-high heat. Sear the halloumi for 2–3 minutes per side until a caramelized crust forms. Remove from heat immediately to maintain a supple interior.

IV. In a small glass bowl, mince the @garlic{1%clove} and whisk with @fresh lemon juice{2%tbsp}, remaining @extra virgin olive oil{3%tbsp}, @dried oregano{1/2%tsp}, @sea salt{1/4%tsp}, and @black pepper{1/4%tsp}. **Crucial Step:** Whisk vigorously to emulsify the lemon juice and oil, ensuring the dressing doesn't separate upon contact with the vegetables.

V. Halve the @cherry tomatoes{1%cup}, dice the @english cucumber{1/2%cup} and @red onion{1/4%cup}, and roughly chop the @fresh flat-leaf parsley{1/4%cup}.

VI. Combine the garden vegetables and toasted bread in a large mixing bowl. Drizzle with the vinaigrette and toss gently.

VII. Top the salad with the warm, seared halloumi slices. Serve immediately while the temperature contrast between the cold vegetables and warm cheese is most pronounced.

# 💡 Chef's Additions & Troubleshooting

*   **The Maillard Reaction:** To maximize flavor, ensure your pan is shimmering hot before adding the halloumi. The chemical reaction between amino acids and reducing sugars creates those savory, browned notes that balance the acidity of the lemon.
*   **Managing Salt Levels:** Halloumi is naturally high in sodium. If you find the dish too salty, increase the @fresh lemon juice{2%tbsp} by an additional teaspoon; the citric acid acts as a flavor foil, neutralizing the perceived saltiness.
*   **Texture Preservation:** Always add the toasted sourdough cubes at the very end or just before serving. The porous nature of sourdough means it will absorb the vinaigrette quickly; adding them too early results in a loss of structural integrity (sogginess).
