A beautiful, light crustless quiche packed with organic fresh garden vegetables and herbs.

# 🥚 Ingredients
- @pasture-raised eggs{6%whole}
- @organic half and half{0.5%cup}
- @cherry tomatoes{1%cup}
- @baby spinach{2%cups}
- @feta cheese{0.5%cup}
- @olive oil{1%tbsp}
- @dried oregano{1%tsp}
- @sea salt{0.5%tsp}

# 🍳 Instructions
I. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C) and grease a 9-inch pie dish or cast-iron skillet with a little olive oil.
II. Heat the @olive oil{1%tbsp} in a pan over medium heat. Sauté the @baby spinach{2%cups} and halved @cherry tomatoes{1%cup} for 2-3 minutes until the spinach is wilted and excess moisture has evaporated.
III. In a large bowl, whisk the @pasture-raised eggs{6%whole} and @organic half and half{0.5%cup} together with @dried oregano{1%tsp} and @sea salt{0.5%tsp} until light and frothy.
IV. Layer the sautéed spinach and tomatoes evenly across the bottom of the prepared pie dish, then sprinkle the crumbled @feta cheese{0.5%cup} on top.
V. Pour the egg mixture slowly over the vegetables and cheese, ensuring they are evenly distributed.
VI. Bake in the oven for 30-35 minutes, or until the center is set and the top is lightly golden. Let cool for 5 minutes before slicing.

💡 Chef's Additions & Troubleshooting
- **Avoiding Soggy Quiche:** Ensure the vegetables are fully sautéed and drained of excess water before adding to the eggs.
- **Cheese Options:** Swap feta for goat cheese or organic shredded cheddar to change the flavor profile.
