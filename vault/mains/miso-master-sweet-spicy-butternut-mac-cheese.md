# 🌿 Miso Master Sweet, Spicy Butternut Mac & Cheese

An elegant, plant-forward reimagining of a comfort classic. This dish balances the deep umami of fermented miso with the natural sweetness of roasted butternut squash and a calibrated spicy finish.

## 🛒 Ingredients

* @brown rice pasta{12%oz}
* @butternut squash, cubed{4%cups}
* @white miso paste{2%tbsp}
* @nutritional yeast{1/4%cup}
* @maple syrup{1%tsp}
* @sriracha{1%tsp}
* @garlic, minced{3%cloves}
* @yellow onion, diced{1/2%cup}
* @unsweetened almond milk{1%cup}
* @extra virgin olive oil{2%tbsp}
* @smoked paprika{1/2%tsp}
* @sea salt{1/2%tsp}
* @black pepper{to taste}

## 🍳 Instructions

I. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C). Toss @butternut squash, cubed{4%cups} with @extra virgin olive oil{1%tbsp}, @sea salt{1/2%tsp}, and @black pepper{to taste} on a parchment-lined sheet pan. Roast for 25–30 minutes until edges are caramelized and tender.

II. Bring a large pot of salted water to a rolling boil. Cook @brown rice pasta{12%oz} until al dente. **Technique Note:** Reserve 1/2 cup of the starchy pasta water before draining the noodles.

III. While the squash roasts, heat the remaining @extra virgin olive oil{1%tbsp} in a pan over medium heat. Sauté @yellow onion, diced{1/2%cup} and @garlic, minced{3%cloves} until translucent and fragrant.

IV. In a high-speed blender, combine the roasted @butternut squash{4%cups}, sautéed onion and garlic, @white miso paste{2%tbsp}, @nutritional yeast{1/4%cup}, @maple syrup{1%tsp}, @sriracha{1%tsp}, @smoked paprika{1/2%tsp}, and @unsweetened almond milk{1%cup}.

V. Process on high until the mixture is completely smooth and velvety. If the consistency is too dense, incrementally blend in the reserved pasta water until a glossy, pourable sauce is achieved.

VI. Return the cooked pasta to the pot over low heat. Pour the miso-butternut sauce over the pasta and toss gently to coat. **Crucial Step:** Avoid bringing the sauce to a hard boil once the miso is added, as extreme heat can diminish the complex flavor profile of the fermented paste.

## 💡 Chef's Additions & Troubleshooting

* **Umami Synergy:** The pairing of white miso and nutritional yeast mimics the chemical structure of aged cheeses. The glutamates in both ingredients work together to create a "savory depth" that tricks the palate into perceiving a traditional dairy richness.
* **Starch Emulsification:** If the sauce separates, it is likely due to a lack of emulsifier. The reserved pasta water contains amylose (starch), which acts as a stabilizer, binding the fats from the olive oil and squash to the aqueous almond milk.
* **Flavor Balancing:** If the sauce feels too "earthy," add a teaspoon of apple cider vinegar or lemon juice. The acidity cuts through the density of the squash and brightens the fermented notes of the miso.
