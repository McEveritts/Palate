# 🌿 Basil Shrimp with Sweet Corn Salad

This vibrant, clean-eating composition balances the natural sweetness of summer corn with the aromatic brightness of fresh basil and perfectly seared shrimp. A refined choice for those seeking a nutrient-dense, professional-grade lunch.

# 🥗 Ingredients

**For the Shrimp:**
- @jumbo shrimp{12%oz}, peeled and deveined
- @extra-virgin olive oil{1%tbsp}
- @garlic clove{2%cloves}, minced
- @fresh basil leaves{1/4%cup}, chiffonade
- @sea salt{1/4%tsp}
- @black pepper{1/8%tsp}

**For the Sweet Corn Salad:**
- @fresh corn kernels{2%cups}, raw or lightly blanched
- @cherry tomatoes{1%cup}, halved
- @red onion{1/4%cup}, finely diced
- @extra-virgin olive oil{1%tbsp}
- @fresh lemon juice{1%tbsp}
- @fresh basil leaves{1/4%cup}, torn
- @sea salt{1/4%tsp}
- @black pepper{1/8%tsp}

# 🍳 Preparation

I. In a chilled mixing bowl, combine the @fresh corn kernels{2%cups}, @cherry tomatoes{1%cup}, and @red onion{1/4%cup}. Whisk together the @extra-virgin olive oil{1%tbsp} and @fresh lemon juice{1%tbsp}, then fold the emulsion into the vegetables along with @sea salt{1/4%tsp} and @black pepper{1/8%tsp}. Gently stir in the torn @fresh basil leaves{1/4%cup} and set aside to macerate.

II. Pat the @jumbo shrimp{12%oz} completely dry with a lint-free towel. Season evenly with @sea salt{1/4%tsp} and @black pepper{1/8%tsp}.

III. Heat @extra-virgin olive oil{1%tbsp} in a stainless steel or cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat. **Technique Note:** Wait until the oil reaches the shimmering point to ensure an immediate sear.

IV. Add the shrimp to the pan in a single layer. Sear for 2 minutes without moving them to develop a golden crust. Flip and add the minced @garlic clove{2%cloves}, sautéing for an additional 1–2 minutes. **Crucial Step:** Remove the pan from the heat immediately once the shrimp are opaque to prevent overcooking and a rubbery texture.

V. Fold in the @fresh basil leaves{1/4%cup} (chiffonade) to the shrimp, allowing the residual heat to wilt the herb slightly and release its essential oils.

VI. Divide the corn salad between two chilled plates and crown each with the warm basil shrimp. Finish with a final drizzle of lemon juice if desired.

# 💡 Chef's Additions & Troubleshooting

*   **The Maillard Reaction:** To achieve the professional golden-brown crust on the shrimp, moisture is the enemy. If the shrimp are damp, they will steam rather than sear, resulting in a greyish hue and loss of flavor depth. Always pat them dry.
*   **Acid-Base Balance:** If the corn is exceptionally sweet, increase the @fresh lemon juice{1%tbsp} by a teaspoon. The acidity cuts through the sugar of the corn and the richness of the olive oil, brightening the overall flavor profile.
*   **Basil Preservation:** Always add the chiffonade basil at the very end of the cooking process. High heat oxidizes the chlorophyll and volatile oils in basil, turning the leaves black and altering the flavor from peppery-sweet to bitter.n added crunch, fold in 1/4 cup of toasted pumpkin seeds (pepitas) to the salad. This introduces a nutty element that complements the sweetness of the corn.
