# 🌿 Ingredients

### The Cabbage Wrap
* @green cabbage{1%head}
* @sea salt{1%tsp}

### The Shepherd's Filling
* @grass-fed ground lamb{1%lb}
* @yellow onion{1%medium, diced}
* @carrots{2%medium, finely diced}
* @frozen organic peas{1/2%cup}
* @garlic{3%cloves, minced}
* @fresh thyme{1%tsp, chopped}
* @fresh rosemary{1%tsp, chopped}
* @extra virgin olive oil{1%tbsp}
* @black pepper{1/2%tsp}

### The Potato Crown
* @Yukon Gold potatoes{1.5%lbs, peeled and cubed}
* @grass-fed ghee{2%tbsp}
* @unsweetened almond milk{1/4%cup}
* @sea salt{1/2%tsp}

### The Guinness Gravy
* @Guinness Draught{1%cup}
* @beef bone broth{1%cup}
* @arrowroot powder{1%tbsp}
* @grass-fed butter{1%tbsp}

# 🍳 Instructions

I. **Prepare the Cabbage:** Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Carefully remove the outer leaves of the @green cabbage{1%head} and blanch them for 2-3 minutes until pliable. Immediately plunge them into an ice bath. **Technique Note:** This "shocking" process preserves the vibrant green color and prevents the leaves from overcooking during the bake.

II. **Sauté the Filling:** Heat @extra virgin olive oil{1%tbsp} in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add @yellow onion{1%medium} and @carrots{2%medium}, sautéing until softened. Stir in @grass-fed ground lamb{1%lb}, browning thoroughly. **Crucial Step:** Allow the lamb to sear without stirring for 3 minutes to initiate the Maillard reaction for deeper flavor. Stir in @garlic{3%cloves}, @fresh thyme{1%tsp}, @fresh rosemary{1%tsp}, and @black pepper{1/2%tsp}. Fold in @frozen organic peas{1/2%cup} and remove from heat.

III. **Assemble the Rolls:** Pat the blanched cabbage leaves dry. Place a generous scoop of the lamb mixture in the center of each leaf. Fold the sides inward and roll tightly. Place the rolls seam-side down in a baking dish.

IV. **Prepare the Crown:** Boil @Yukon Gold potatoes{1.5%lbs} until tender. Drain and mash with @grass-fed ghee{2%tbsp}, @unsweetened almond milk{1/4%cup}, and @sea salt{1/2%tsp} until smooth. Carefully spoon the mash over the top of each cabbage roll.

V. **Reduce the Gravy:** In a small saucepan, simmer @Guinness Draught{1%cup} and @beef bone broth{1%cup} until reduced by one-third. Whisk @arrowroot powder{1%tbsp} with a tablespoon of water to create a slurry, then stir into the simmering liquid. Finish by whisking in @grass-fed butter{1%tbsp} for a glossy sheen.

VI. **Final Bake:** Pour the Guinness gravy around the base of the rolls (not over the potatoes). Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 25-30 minutes until the potato tops are golden brown.

# 💡 Chef's Additions & Troubleshooting

* **The Starch Balance:** If the gravy is too thin, avoid adding more thickener directly to the pan, as it may clump. Always use a "slurry" (cold liquid + starch) to ensure a homogenous emulsion.
* **Cabbage Texture:** To avoid "watery" rolls, ensure cabbage leaves are thoroughly patted dry after blanching. Excess surface moisture creates steam inside the roll, which can dilute the flavor of the lamb filling.
* **Flavor Depth:** For an extra layer of complexity, deglaze the lamb skillet with a splash of the Guinness before adding the meat to the cabbage; this captures the fond (browned bits) and integrates the stout flavor throughout the entire dish.
