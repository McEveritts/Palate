# 🌿 Veggie Coconut Soup

A refined, clean-eating blend of aromatic roots and creamy coconut, designed to cleanse the palate and nourish the body.

# 🥕 Ingredients

* @unrefined coconut oil{1%tbsp}
* @yellow onion{1%small, diced}
* @fresh ginger{1%tbsp, grated}
* @garlic cloves{3%minced}
* @turmeric powder{1%tsp}
* @carrots{2%medium, sliced}
* @red bell pepper{1%medium, diced}
* @vegetable broth{4%cups}
* @full-fat coconut milk{14%oz}
* @snap peas{1%cup}
* @fresh lime juice{1%tbsp}
* @fresh cilantro{2%tbsp, chopped}

# 🍳 Instructions

I. Heat @unrefined coconut oil{1%tbsp} in a heavy-bottomed pot over medium heat. Sauté @yellow onion{1%small, diced} until translucent and fragrant.

II. Stir in @fresh ginger{1%tbsp, grated}, @garlic cloves{3%minced}, and @turmeric powder{1%tsp}. **Technique Note:** Bloom the spices for 60 seconds to release fat-soluble aromatic compounds without scorching the garlic.

III. Incorporate @carrots{2%medium, sliced} and @red bell pepper{1%medium, diced}, stirring to coat the vegetables in the golden aromatic base.

IV. Pour in @vegetable broth{4%cups} and bring to a gentle simmer. Reduce heat and cook for 10–12 minutes until the carrots are fork-tender.

V. Lower the heat to low and stir in @full-fat coconut milk{14%oz}. **Crucial Step:** Avoid bringing the soup to a rolling boil once the coconut milk is added to prevent the emulsion from breaking and the fats from separating.

VI. Gently fold in @snap peas{1%cup} and simmer for an additional 2 minutes to preserve their vibrant green color and snap.

VII. Remove from heat and stir in @fresh lime juice{1%tbsp}. Garnish with @fresh cilantro{2%tbsp, chopped} before serving.

# 💡 Chef's Additions & Troubleshooting

* **The Acid-Fat Balance:** The richness of the coconut milk requires a precise acidic counterpoint. If the soup feels "heavy" on the palate, incrementally increase the lime juice to brighten the flavor profile and cut through the lipids.
* **Emulsion Stability:** To ensure a velvety texture, use a whisk when incorporating the coconut milk. If the soup separates, whisking on low heat will re-incorporate the fats into the aqueous broth.
* **Texture Modification:** For a more luxurious, thickened consistency, remove one cup of the simmered carrots and broth, blend until smooth, and stir back into the pot before adding the coconut milk.
