# 🌿 Halloumi Prosciutto Bites

An elegant fusion of salty, sweet, and savory. These bites pair the firmness of Mediterranean halloumi with the delicate saltiness of cured prosciutto and the natural sweetness of fresh figs.

## 🛒 Ingredients

* @halloumi cheese{250%g}
* @prosciutto di parma{6%slices}
* @fresh figs{4%pieces}
* @raw honey{1%tbsp}
* @extra virgin olive oil{1%tsp}
* @fresh thyme{1%sprig}
* @black pepper{1%pinch}

## 🍳 Instructions

I. Slice the @halloumi cheese{250%g} into uniform 1-inch cubes. Pat them dry with a paper towel to ensure a superior sear.

II. Heat @extra virgin olive oil{1%tsp} in a non-stick skillet over medium-high heat. **Technique Note:** Ensure the oil is shimmering but not smoking before adding the cheese to prevent sticking and ensure an even golden crust.

III. Carefully place the halloumi cubes in the pan. Sear for 1–2 minutes per side until deeply golden brown. Remove from heat and let cool for 60 seconds.

IV. Cut each @prosciutto di parma{6%slices} into thirds. Wrap a strip of prosciutto around each seared halloumi cube, securing it with the natural tackiness of the meat.

V. Quarter the @fresh figs{4%pieces}. Place one fig wedge atop each wrapped cube. 

VI. **Crucial Step:** Using a spoon or drizzle bottle, apply a small amount of @raw honey{1%tbsp} over the fig. The honey acts as a culinary adhesive and a flavor balancer against the salt.

VII. Finish with a @black pepper{1%pinch} and a few leaves of @fresh thyme{1%sprig} for aromatic brightness.

## 💡 Chef's Additions & Troubleshooting

* **The Maillard Reaction:** The deep brown crust on the halloumi is the result of the Maillard reaction. To maximize this, avoid crowding the pan; if the cubes are too close, they will steam rather than sear, resulting in a rubbery texture instead of a crisp exterior.
* **Managing Salinity:** Both halloumi and prosciutto are high in sodium. To balance the palate, the inclusion of fresh figs provides an enzymatic sweetness and acidity that cuts through the richness. If the bites feel too salty, add a drop of fresh lemon juice to the honey.
* **Structural Integrity:** If the prosciutto does not stay in place, lightly toast the wrapped bite in the pan for 30 seconds. The heat will render the fat in the prosciutto, causing it to shrink and "cling" tightly to the cheese.
