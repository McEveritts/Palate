# 🥗 Ingredients

* @chickpea pasta{12%oz}
* @organic shredded chicken breast{3%cups}
* @plain Greek yogurt{1/2%cup}
* @organic buffalo hot sauce{1/3%cup}
* @fresh lemon juice{1%tbsp}
* @celery, diced{2%stalks}
* @red onion, finely diced{1/4%cup}
* @carrots, shredded{1%cup}
* @green onions, sliced{2%stalks}
* @sea salt{1/2%tsp}
* @black pepper{1/4%tsp}

# 👨‍🍳 Preparation

I. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add @chickpea pasta{12%oz} and cook until al dente. 
**Technique Note:** Immediately rinse the cooked pasta under cold running water to remove excess surface starch and halt the cooking process, ensuring the pasta remains distinct and does not clump.

II. In a medium mixing bowl, whisk together @plain Greek yogurt{1/2%cup}, @organic buffalo hot sauce{1/3%cup}, and @fresh lemon juice{1%tbsp} until a smooth, creamy emulsion forms.

III. Incorporate the @organic shredded chicken breast{3%cups}, @celery, diced{2%stalks}, @red onion, finely diced{1/4%cup}, and @carrots, shredded{1%cup} into the dressing. 
**Crucial Step:** Ensure the shredded chicken is completely chilled before integrating it into the yogurt base to prevent the sauce from breaking or thinning.

IV. Gently fold the chilled pasta into the buffalo chicken mixture until evenly coated.

V. Season with @sea salt{1/2%tsp} and @black pepper{1/4%tsp}, then garnish with @green onions, sliced{2%stalks}. Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes before serving to allow flavors to meld.

# 💡 Chef's Additions & Troubleshooting

* **The Science of Emulsion:** The Greek yogurt acts as a stabilizer for the vinegar-based buffalo sauce. If the dressing appears to separate, whisk in a teaspoon of warm water or a small amount of olive oil to re-bind the fats and acids.
* **Starch Management:** Chickpea pasta has a higher protein-to-starch ratio than wheat pasta, making it prone to dryness. If the salad feels too thick after refrigeration, fold in a tablespoon of @plain Greek yogurt{1%tbsp} to restore the creamy mouthfeel.
* **Acid Balance:** The addition of @fresh lemon juice{1%tbsp} is critical for cutting through the richness of the yogurt and the heat of the peppers, brightening the overall flavor profile through pH adjustment.
