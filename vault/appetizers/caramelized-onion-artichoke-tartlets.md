Savory, delicate bite-sized tartlets filled with rich caramelized sweet onions and tender marinated artichokes.

# 🥐 Ingredients
- @puff pastry sheet{1%whole}
- @yellow onions{2%whole}
- @marinated artichoke hearts{1%cup}
- @goat cheese{4%oz}
- @olive oil{1.5%tbsp}
- @fresh thyme{2%sprigs}
- @balsamic vinegar{1%tbsp}

# 🍳 Instructions
I. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
II. Slice the @yellow onions{2%whole} into thin rings. Heat @olive oil{1.5%tbsp} in a large skillet over medium-low heat. Add onions and cook slowly, stirring occasionally, for 20-25 minutes until deep golden brown. Stir in the @balsamic vinegar{1%tbsp} in the last minute.
III. Roll out the thawed @puff pastry sheet{1%whole} on a lightly floured surface. Cut into 12 small squares or rounds and place them on the baking sheet. Lightly score a border around each.
IV. Drain and chop the @marinated artichoke hearts{1%cup}.
V. Distribute a small spoonful of caramelized onions in the center of each puff pastry square, then top with chopped artichokes and crumbled @goat cheese{4%oz}.
VI. Sprinkle with fresh @fresh thyme{2%sprigs} leaves. Bake for 12-15 minutes until the pastry is puffed and golden brown. Serve warm.

💡 Chef's Additions & Troubleshooting
- **Pastry Prep:** Ensure the puff pastry is cold when going into the oven to get the maximum rise and flaky texture.
- **Onion Short-cut:** If short on time, caramelized onions can be prepared ahead and stored in the fridge for up to 3 days.
