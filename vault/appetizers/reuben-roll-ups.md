# 🌿 Reuben Roll Ups
A refined, clean-eating interpretation of the classic deli sandwich, reimagined as an elegant appetizer. This version prioritizes fermented probiotics and sprouted grains while maintaining the bold, savory profile of a traditional Reuben.

# 🥖 Ingredients
- @sprouted grain wraps{4%units}
- @lean corned beef{12%slices}
- @organic Swiss cheese{4%slices}
- @fermented sauerkraut{1%cup}
- @Greek yogurt{1/4%cup}
- @organic tomato paste{1%tbsp}
- @Dijon mustard{1%tsp}
- @lemon juice{1%tsp}
- @paprika{1/4%tsp}
- @extra virgin olive oil{1%tbsp}

# 🍳 Preparation

I. **Prepare the Clean-Russian Sauce:** In a small mixing bowl, whisk together @Greek yogurt{1/4%cup}, @organic tomato paste{1%tbsp}, @Dijon mustard{1%tsp}, @lemon juice{1%tsp}, and @paprika{1/4%tsp} until emulsified and smooth. Set aside.

II. **Prep the Filling:** **Crucial Step:** Use a clean kitchen towel or paper towels to squeeze all excess moisture from the @fermented sauerkraut{1%cup}. This prevents the wraps from becoming soggy.

III. **Assemble the Rolls:** Lay the @sprouted grain wraps{4%units} flat on a workspace. Spread a thin, even layer of the prepared sauce across the surface of each wrap. 

IV. **Layering:** Place three @lean corned beef{12%slices} slices on each wrap, followed by one @organic Swiss cheese{4%slices} slice (torn to fit the wrap). Top with a centered line of the dried sauerkraut.

V. **The Roll:** Fold the sides of the wrap inward slightly and roll tightly from the bottom up to create a secure cylinder.

VI. **The Sear:** Heat @extra virgin olive oil{1%tbsp} in a non-stick skillet over medium-high heat. **Technique Note:** Place the roll-ups seam-side down first to "lock" the wrap in place. Sear for 60–90 seconds per side until the exterior is golden-brown and the cheese has reached a soft melt.

VII. **Finish:** Slice each roll into 3 equal pieces diagonally for a professional presentation.

# 💡 Chef's Additions & Troubleshooting

*   **Moisture Migration & Texture:** The primary challenge with Reuben roll-ups is the water content of the sauerkraut. By removing the brine and searing the seam first, you create a structural seal that prevents the internal steam from softening the sprouted grain wrap.
*   **The Maillard Reaction:** Ensure the pan is shimmering hot before adding the rolls. Achieving a quick, high-heat sear triggers the Maillard reaction on the grain wrap, adding a nutty, toasted flavor that complements the saltiness of the corned beef.
*   **Probiotic Integrity:** To preserve the live cultures in the fermented sauerkraut, avoid overcooking the interior. The goal is a warmed center and a crisp exterior; internal temperatures should stay below 115°F to maintain the probiotic benefits.
