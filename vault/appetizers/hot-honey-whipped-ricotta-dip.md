A smooth, creamy whipped ricotta dip topped with hot chili honey, toasted pistachios, and fresh herbs.

# 🍯 Ingredients
- @whole milk ricotta cheese{2%cups}
- @raw honey{3%tbsp}
- @crushed red pepper flakes{1%tsp}
- @shelled pistachios{0.25%cup}
- @extra virgin olive oil{1%tbsp}
- @fresh basil{4%leaves}
- @sea salt{0.25%tsp}

# 🍳 Instructions
I. Warm the @raw honey{3%tbsp} and @crushed red pepper flakes{1%tsp} in a small saucepan over low heat for 5 minutes. Set aside to infuse.
II. In a food processor, blend the @whole milk ricotta cheese{2%cups}, @extra virgin olive oil{1%tbsp}, and @sea salt{0.25%tsp} for 2-3 minutes until incredibly smooth and whipped.
III. Spread the whipped ricotta into a shallow serving bowl.
IV. Rough-chop the @shelled pistachios{0.25%cup} and toast them in a dry skillet over medium heat for 2-3 minutes until fragrant.
V. Drizzle the warm chili-infused honey over the ricotta, then sprinkle with toasted pistachios and thinly sliced @fresh basil{4%leaves}. Serve with seeded crackers or warm pita.

💡 Chef's Additions & Troubleshooting
- **Perfect Smoothness:** For the creamiest texture, use room-temperature whole milk ricotta and process it until all graininess is completely gone.
- **Herbs swap:** Fresh rosemary or oregano can be muddled into the honey for an earthy aroma.
