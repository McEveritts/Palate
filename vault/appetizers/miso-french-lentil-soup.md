# 🌿 Miso French Lentil Soup

An elegant fusion of French terroir and Japanese umami. This clean-eating soup utilizes the structural integrity of Puy lentils paired with the probiotic depth of white miso for a sophisticated, nutrient-dense appetizer.

## 🛒 Ingredients

* @extra virgin olive oil{2%tbsp}
* @yellow onion, diced{1%medium}
* @carrots, diced{2%medium}
* @celery, diced{2%stalks}
* @garlic, minced{3%cloves}
* @fresh ginger, grated{1%tbsp}
* @French green lentils, rinsed{1%cup}
* @low-sodium vegetable broth{6%cups}
* @white miso paste{3%tbsp}
* @baby spinach{2%cups}
* @fresh lemon juice{1%tbsp}
* @black pepper{1%pinch}

## 👨‍🍳 Instructions

I. Heat @extra virgin olive oil{2%tbsp} in a heavy-bottomed pot over medium heat. Add @yellow onion, diced{1%medium}, @carrots, diced{2%medium}, and @celery, diced{2%stalks}. Sauté for 6–8 minutes until softened.

II. Stir in @garlic, minced{3%cloves} and @fresh ginger, grated{1%tbsp}. Cook for an additional 60 seconds until fragrant.

III. Incorporate @French green lentils, rinsed{1%cup} and @low-sodium vegetable broth{6%cups}. Bring to a gentle boil, then reduce heat to low. Cover and simmer for 25–30 minutes. 
**Crucial Step:** Ensure the lentils are tender but still maintain a slight "al dente" bite to prevent the soup from becoming porridge-like.

IV. Remove the pot from heat. Place @white miso paste{3%tbsp} into a small strainer or bowl and whisk in a ladle of the hot broth until smooth, then stir the slurry back into the main pot. 
**Technique Note:** Never boil miso paste directly. High temperatures destroy the delicate probiotics and alter the nuanced flavor profile of the fermented soy.

V. Fold in @baby spinach{2%cups} and @fresh lemon juice{1%tbsp}. Stir gently until the spinach is just wilted. Season with @black pepper{1%pinch} to taste.

## 💡 Chef's Additions & Troubleshooting

*   **The Science of Saponins:** If you find the lentils are taking too long to soften, avoid adding salt or acidic components (like lemon juice) at the beginning. Sodium and acid can strengthen the pectin in the lentil skins, hindering the softening process. Always add the miso and lemon at the end.
*   **Umami Balancing:** If the miso flavor feels too dominant, introduce a teaspoon of maple syrup or a splash of apple cider vinegar. This creates a flavor bridge between the salty fermentation of the miso and the earthy sweetness of the French lentils.
*   **Texture Refinement:** For a creamier mouthfeel without adding dairy, take one cup of the finished soup (including lentils), blend it until smooth, and stir it back into the pot. This emulsifies the broth while keeping the rustic aesthetic.
