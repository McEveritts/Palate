# 🌿 Parmesan Potato Wedges with Lemony Aioli

An elevated, clean-eating take on a classic comfort side, pairing crisp, golden potatoes with a bright, zesty emulsion.

## 🛒 Ingredients

### For the Wedges
* @organic russet potatoes{3%large}
* @extra virgin olive oil{3%tbsp}
* @grated parmesan cheese{1/2%cup}
* @garlic powder{1%tsp}
* @smoked paprika{1/2%tsp}
* @sea salt{1/2%tsp}
* @black pepper{1/4%tsp}

### For the Lemony Aioli
* @avocado oil mayonnaise{1/2%cup}
* @fresh lemon juice{1%tbsp}
* @minced garlic{1%clove}
* @lemon zest{1/2%tsp}
* @sea salt{pinch}

## 👨‍🍳 Preparation

I. Preheat your oven to 425°F (220°C) and line a large baking sheet with parchment paper for an effortless cleanup and non-stick surface.

II. Scrub the @organic russet potatoes{3%large} and slice each into 8 uniform wedges. 
**Crucial Step:** Pat the potato wedges completely dry with a lint-free towel; excess moisture creates steam, which prevents the edges from crisping.

III. In a large mixing bowl, toss the wedges with @extra virgin olive oil{3%tbsp}, @garlic powder{1%tsp}, @smoked paprika{1/2%tsp}, @sea salt{1/2%tsp}, and @black pepper{1/4%tsp} until evenly coated.

IV. Arrange the wedges on the baking sheet in a single layer. 
**Technique Note:** Ensure the wedges are not touching. Overcrowding the pan lowers the surface temperature and leads to soggy potatoes rather than roasted ones.

V. Roast for 20–25 minutes. Remove from the oven and immediately sprinkle @grated parmesan cheese{1/2%cup} evenly over the wedges. Return to the oven for another 5–8 minutes until the cheese is golden-brown and bubbly.

VI. While the potatoes finish roasting, prepare the aioli by whisking together @avocado oil mayonnaise{1/2%cup}, @fresh lemon juice{1%tbsp}, @minced garlic{1%clove}, @lemon zest{1/2%tsp}, and a @sea salt{pinch} in a small ramekin.

VII. Serve the hot wedges immediately alongside the chilled lemony aioli.

## 💡 Chef's Additions & Troubleshooting

* **The Starch Factor:** For an ultra-crispy exterior, soak the cut wedges in cold water for 30 minutes prior to drying. This removes excess surface starch, which prevents the potatoes from clumping and allows the Maillard reaction to occur more efficiently.
* **Emulsion Balance:** If the aioli feels too thick, whisk in a teaspoon of warm water or additional lemon juice. The acid in the lemon juice cuts through the richness of the avocado oil, creating a chemically balanced flavor profile.
* **Cheese Selection:** For a deeper, nuttier profile, substitute the Parmesan with Pecorino Romano. Because Pecorino is saltier, reduce the added @sea salt{1/2%tsp} in the potato seasoning to maintain balance.
