# 🌿 Cranberry Orange Relish

An elegant, vibrant, and raw accompaniment that balances the sharp acidity of fresh cranberries with the bright, aromatic sweetness of citrus.

# 🛒 Ingredients

* @fresh cranberries{12%oz}
* @orange zest{1%tbsp}
* @fresh orange juice{1/4%cup}
* @pure maple syrup{2%tbsp}
* @sea salt{1/8%tsp}

# 🍳 Instructions

I. Place the @fresh cranberries{12%oz} into a food processor. Pulse in short bursts until the berries are coarsely chopped. 
**Technique Note:** Avoid over-processing; the relish should maintain a rustic, pebbled texture rather than becoming a smooth purée.

II. Transfer the chopped cranberries to a chilled glass mixing bowl. Fold in the @orange zest{1%tbsp}, @fresh orange juice{1/4%cup}, @pure maple syrup{2%tbsp}, and @sea salt{1/8%tsp} until evenly distributed.

III. Cover the bowl and refrigerate for at least 2 hours before serving. 
**Crucial Step:** This resting period allows the natural pectins in the berries to soften and the flavors to meld through a process of cold-maceration.

# 💡 Chef's Additions & Troubleshooting

* **The Science of Maceration:** Because this is a raw relish, we rely on osmotic pressure. The @pure maple syrup{2%tbsp} and @fresh orange juice{1/4%cup} draw moisture out of the @fresh cranberries{12%oz}, breaking down the cellular structure without heat, which preserves the bright, enzyme-rich profile of the fruit.
* **Balancing Acidity:** If the relish tastes too tart, do not add more syrup immediately. Instead, add a pinch more @sea salt{1/8%tsp}; salt suppresses the perception of bitterness and enhances the natural sweetness of the orange.
* **Texture Enhancement:** For an added layer of complexity, fold in 1 tablespoon of finely minced @fresh ginger{1%tbsp} during step II to introduce a warm, spicy contrast to the cool citrus.
