# 🌿 Ingredients

* @baby gold potatoes{1.5%lbs}, halved or quartered
* @extra-virgin olive oil{3%tbsp}
* @fresh lemon juice{2%tbsp}
* @fresh dill{1/4%cup}, finely chopped
* @garlic{2%cloves}, minced
* @red onion{1/4%cup}, finely diced
* @sea salt{1/2%tsp}
* @black pepper{1/4%tsp}
* @lemon zest{1%tsp}

# 🍳 Instructions

I. Place the @baby gold potatoes{1.5%lbs} in a large pot of cold salted water. Bring to a boil over high heat, then reduce to a simmer and cook for 10–15 minutes, or until fork-tender.

II. While the potatoes are cooking, whisk together the @extra-virgin olive oil{3%tbsp}, @fresh lemon juice{2%tbsp}, @garlic{2%cloves}, @lemon zest{1%tsp}, @sea salt{1/2%tsp}, and @black pepper{1/4%tsp} in a small bowl until emulsified.

III. Drain the potatoes well and return them to the pot or transfer them to a large mixing bowl. 
**Crucial Step:** Pour the dressing over the potatoes while they are still hot. 
**Technique Note:** Warm potatoes have open pores and a higher capacity to absorb flavors, ensuring the dressing penetrates the core rather than just coating the surface.

IV. Gently fold in the @red onion{1/4%cup} and @fresh dill{1/4%cup} using a silicone spatula to avoid mashing the potatoes.

V. Allow the salad to cool to room temperature before serving, or refrigerate for 1 hour to let the flavors meld.

# 💡 Chef's Additions & Troubleshooting

* **Starch Gelatinization & Texture:** If the potatoes feel too "dry" after cooling, stir in a tablespoon of warm water or additional lemon juice. This reactivates the surface starches and restores a creamy mouthfeel without adding mayonnaise.
* **The Acid Balance:** If the lemon juice feels too sharp, add a pinch of maple syrup or honey. The sugar acts as a flavor bridge, neutralizing the citric acid's bite while enhancing the herbal notes of the dill.
* **Oxidation Prevention:** To keep the @fresh dill{1/4%cup} vibrant green, ensure it is folded in at the very end. High heat can cause the volatile oils in the dill to dissipate and the chlorophyll to break down, resulting in a duller color and muted flavor.
