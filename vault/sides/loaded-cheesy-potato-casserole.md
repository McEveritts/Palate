# 🥔 Ingredients

- @Yukon Gold potatoes{4%large, peeled and thinly sliced}
- @grass-fed butter{3%tbsp, melted}
- @garlic cloves{3%cloves, minced}
- @plain Greek yogurt{1/2%cup}
- @sharp grass-fed cheddar{2%cups, shredded}
- @fresh chives{3%tbsp, chopped}
- @sea salt{1%tsp}
- @black pepper{1/2%tsp}
- @smoked paprika{1/4%tsp}

# 👨‍🍳 Preparation

I. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C). Lightly grease a 9x9 inch baking dish with a small amount of @grass-fed butter{3%tbsp}.

II. In a large mixing bowl, combine the sliced @Yukon Gold potatoes{4%large} with melted @grass-fed butter{3%tbsp}, @garlic cloves{3%cloves}, @sea salt{1%tsp}, @black pepper{1/2%tsp}, and @smoked paprika{1/4%tsp}. **Technique Note:** Tossing the potatoes in fat before layering creates a hydrophobic barrier that prevents the slices from sticking together and ensures an even sear.

III. In a small ramekin, whisk together the @plain Greek yogurt{1/2%cup} and one cup of the @sharp grass-fed cheddar{2%cups} until smooth.

IV. Layer half of the seasoned potato mixture into the prepared baking dish. Spread the yogurt-cheese mixture evenly across the potato layer.

V. Arrange the remaining potato slices over the cheese sauce. **Crucial Step:** Gently press the top layer down with a spatula to ensure maximum contact with the sauce; this prevents the potatoes from steaming and encourages a caramelized crust.

VI. Cover the dish with foil and bake for 30 minutes. Remove the foil, sprinkle the remaining @sharp grass-fed cheddar{2%cups} over the top, and bake for an additional 15-20 minutes until the cheese is bubbling and golden.

VII. Remove from oven and garnish with @fresh chives{3%tbsp} immediately before serving.

# 💡 Chef's Additions & Troubleshooting

- **Starch Management:** If the casserole appears too watery, it is due to excess surface starch and moisture. To prevent this, you may par-boil the potato slices for 3 minutes in salted water before layering; this gelatinizes the starch and results in a creamier interior.
- **Maillard Reaction:** For a professional-grade golden crust, switch your oven to the 'broil' setting for the final 2 minutes of cooking. This concentrates the heat on the surface, accelerating the Maillard reaction between the amino acids and lactose in the cheese.
- **Acid Balance:** If the dish feels overly rich or "heavy" on the palate, whisk a teaspoon of lemon juice or apple cider vinegar into the @plain Greek yogurt{1/2%cup}. The acidity cuts through the lipids of the butter and cheese, brightening the overall flavor profile.
